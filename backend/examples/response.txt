At a previous job, I was working on a product feature that required close collaboration with a frontend engineer. We had a tight deadline, and there were differing opinions on the API design—specifically on how much logic should live on the client side versus the backend.
Our goal was to deliver a seamless user experience, but we needed to align on a technical approach quickly. The conflict was slowing down progress and creating friction during standups.
I scheduled a one-on-one with the engineer to step away from the group pressure and understand their perspective. I listened to their concerns—mainly around frontend responsiveness—and shared my backend performance concerns. Together, we whiteboarded a hybrid solution: pushing some lightweight logic to the client while keeping the more complex processing server-side. We also agreed to document the API contract clearly to prevent misunderstandings.
We implemented the solution on time, and the performance met both our goals. More importantly, our working relationship improved, and future discussions were more collaborative. The feature shipped with minimal bugs and received positive user feedback.
